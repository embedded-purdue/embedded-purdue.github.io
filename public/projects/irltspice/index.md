# IRLTSPICE

IRLTSPICE is a configurable analog circuit that brings the ease of digital to the power of analog. Draw a circuit, then compile and flash it to the board, and your exact design is instantly created in real life—no simulations and no breadboarding. Akin to an FPGA, but for analog.

- C and C++
