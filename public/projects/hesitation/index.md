# Hesitation

Hesitation is a Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP) testbed built to mimic and research the SR-1 missions, combining propulsion hardware, controls, and embedded systems into a single research platform.

---

## Technologies

- FPGA and microcontrollers
- Propulsion systems
- Physics and mechanical design
- RTOS
- Controls and a centralized neural network (CNN)
